ODRL defines three types of rules that are used to formulate a policy:

1. Permissions: ODRL simply defines this as "the ability to perform an Action over an Asset". These actions are often rights that the rights holder (creator or owner) of the Asset can perform, and can grant to third parties.
2. Prohibitions. These are defined by ODRL as "the inability to perform an Action over an Asset.". In practical terms, these are often rights or actions that the rights holder, subject of sensitive data in the Asset, or curator of the Asset do not grant to a third party.
3. Duty: As defined by ODRL, this is "the obligation to perform an Action". Duties are often related to other actions, especially Permissions, in the sense that a Permission is granted subject to performance of a Duty. For example, use of an Asset may be granted subject to the Duty of proper citation (attribution).

## Extension Types

OpenREL makes no extensions to these classes, except to add mappings and additional usage notes to theor definitions to aid with implementation and crosswalks.

*The scope of this document is as follows:*

2. *In some cases, specifications can be included into this document, especially if the scope is unlikely to fall outside the system or its components in scope.*   
3. *Some specifications, though, are collected in stand-alone documents since they apply across multiple systems or components, or they are maintained externally.*
